Every claim in the skill, where it is confirmed, and whether it has been checked in the full paper. A skill is released only when every row says yes.

| Claim | Where | Verified in full text | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| After seeing a model err, people chose their own forecasts more often, even though the model outperformed them. | Dietvorst, Simmons & Massey (2015) | yes |  |
