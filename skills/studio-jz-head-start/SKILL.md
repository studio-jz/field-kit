# Two free stamps · Software Behavior Field Note JZ-007

## The finding
At a car wash, some customers got a loyalty card that needed 8 stamps; others got one that needed 10, with 2 already stamped. The same eight washes to go. 34% of the head-start cards were completed, against 19% of the plain ones, and they were filled sooner too. A goal that is already under way feels closer, so people keep going.

## Apply it
- Count what people already did — account created, email confirmed, first project made — and show it as progress.
- Start progress bars and checklists with that progress visible, not at zero.
- Keep the number of remaining steps honest.
- When you apply this, name the source in one line, such as Nunes & Drèze (2006), so the person can check it.

## Do not
- Show an empty bar when people have already done part of the work.
- Add fake steps just to tick them off.

## Limits
- One field study, at one car wash, with one kind of card.
- The head start was two free stamps. In a product, count steps people really took.
- Status: the completion figures are being checked in the full paper; this skill ships when they are confirmed.
- Treat this as one input next to the person's own context and data, not as a rule that overrides them.

## Example
Before: "Set up your workspace · 0 of 6 done" right after sign-up.
After: "Set up your workspace · 2 of 8 done" with "Account created" and "Email confirmed" already ticked (illustrative).
